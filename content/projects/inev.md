## Overview

The official website of the **Church of the Annunciation in Nea Ionia, Volos**, developed as a central digital information and communication platform for the parish.

The website provides visitors with access to parish news, announcements, activities, ministries and information about the life of the church, while giving administrators a straightforward way to continuously publish and manage new content.

## The challenge

A parish website contains a significant amount of information that changes frequently.

Beyond presenting the church itself, the platform needed to support the continuous publication of news and announcements while organising information about parish activities, ministries and initiatives in a way that remains accessible to visitors.

The system also had to be simple enough for everyday content management without requiring technical knowledge.

## The solution

The website was developed using **WordPress**, providing the parish with a flexible content management environment for maintaining both permanent information and frequently updated content.

The site's structure organises the different areas of parish life into clearly defined sections while keeping recent news and announcements easily accessible from the homepage.

## News & publishing

A central part of the platform is its publishing functionality.

Through WordPress, administrators can publish and manage:

- Parish news
- Announcements
- Events and activities
- Articles
- Liturgical and pastoral information
- Community initiatives

This allows the website to function not simply as a static presentation of the church, but as an active digital communication channel for the parish.

## Content organisation

The website brings together information covering multiple aspects of parish life, including the history and identity of the church, its ministries, charitable activities, parish organisations and community initiatives.

Content is organised into dedicated sections and categories, making a relatively large amount of information easier to navigate.

## Content management

WordPress provides the underlying CMS, allowing authorised users to update the website, publish new articles and maintain existing pages through an administrative interface.

This makes the platform sustainable for everyday use by the parish without requiring developer involvement for routine content updates.

## Responsive experience

The website is designed to remain accessible across desktop, tablet and mobile devices, allowing parishioners and visitors to access news and information regardless of the device they are using.

## Technology

The platform is built around the **WordPress ecosystem**, with PHP and MySQL powering the application and content management layer, alongside HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the presentation and interactive elements.

## Result

The result is a central online presence for the parish that combines institutional information with an actively maintained news and publishing platform.

Rather than functioning solely as a presentation website, it provides the parish with an ongoing digital channel for communicating its activities, announcements and community life.
